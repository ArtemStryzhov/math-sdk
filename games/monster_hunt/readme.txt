MONSTER HUNT GAME

A 5-reel, 5-row payline slot game featuring expanding Monster symbols and Golden Carrot wilds.

GAME MECHANICS:
- Rabbit symbol (S) expands upward each spin when participating in winning combinations
- Golden Carrot (W) is a Wild symbol with multipliers
- If Rabbit devours a Golden Carrot during expansion, the carrot's multiplier multiplies the Rabbit's multiplier
- Each reel can have only 1 Rabbit symbol at a time
- Bonus symbols (B) can only appear in base game

PAYTABLE:
Low symbols (L1-L5): 3-of-a-kind: $0.20-$0.50, 4-of-a-kind: $0.50-$1.00, 5-of-a-kind: $2.00-$5.00
High symbols (H1-H4): 3-of-a-kind: $1.00-$2.00, 4-of-a-kind: $2.00-$10.00, 5-of-a-kind: $8.00-$20.00
Wild (W): 5-of-a-kind: $25.00
Scatter (S): 5-of-a-kind: $25.00

MULTIPLIERS:
- Wild symbols: x2, x3, x4, x5, or x10
- Scatter symbols: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x15, or x20

BONUS GAMES:
- 3 Bonus symbols: Carrot Ambush Bonus (10 free spins, higher S/W frequency)
- 4 Bonus symbols: Ninjutsu Rabbit Reign Bonus (10 free spins, higher S/W frequency)
- In bonus games, S symbols become sticky and can expand on every spin

WAYS TO WIN:
- 15 predefined paylines starting from leftmost reel
- Only highest base win per line is paid
- Wins require at least 3 matching symbols on adjacent reels

BONUS BUY:
- 3 bonus symbols mode: x100 bet
- 4 bonus symbols mode: x300 bet

TARGET RTP: 96-98%
